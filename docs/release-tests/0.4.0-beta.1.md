## Part 3: Details

Pending: each scenario gets its details here once it has run.

**Row 19, explicit fixed withdraw fee on RIN** (W1, nothing broadcast, 2026-11-05). KDF Reloaded at
66011cdcb, Komodo 2.6.0-beta and GLEEC 3.0.0-beta built the same withdrawals from the same coins
with `UtxoFixed` and `UtxoPerKbyte` at 0.00001234 RIN per 1,000 bytes. On every binary the two fee
types gave the same fee. A 4-input withdrawal cost 825 satoshi on KDF Reloaded and 826 on Komodo
and GLEEC; a 13-input one cost 2,469 and 2,470. Before PR #226 KDF Reloaded would have charged 1,234
and 3,702. *Result:* PASS. Both fee types are proportional, as on Komodo and GLEEC. KDF Reloaded
rounds the change output's share separately, which here costs 1 satoshi less.

## Findings and dispositions

| # | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Row 19: KDF Reloaded rounds the fee share of a change output separately and charges up to 1 base unit less than Komodo and GLEEC KDF, which round once over the whole size. | Parity fix in a follow-up PR (CRD ch.38 R38.6.6b, T38.6.6b.1). |
